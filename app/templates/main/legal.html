Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026.

Ami Goans ("we", "us") is a community platform for Goans around the world. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a securely hashed password if you sign up directly (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Social sign-in: if you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that provider.
  • Profile information: anything you choose to add to your profile, including an uploaded photo, mobile number, and SMS marketing preference.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe to updates.

How we use it

We use your data to run your account, show your profile within the community, and, only with your explicit consent, send SMS offers from Ami Goans or its advertisers. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in, plus a CSRF security token on forms. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies. See the Cookie Policy below for details.

Data retention and your rights

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can update your profile at any time from My Profile. To request a copy of your data, or to have your account and data deleted, email customerservices@amigoans.co.uk — we'll action deletion requests within 30 days.

Cookie Policy

Ami Goans uses only the cookies necessary to run the site:

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in between page visits. Deleted when you sign out or it expires.
  • CSRF token: a security measure to protect forms (like sign-up and profile updates) from being submitted by malicious third-party sites.

We do not currently use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

Terms of Use

  • You must provide accurate information when creating an account or submitting a business, event or job listing.
  • Content you submit (stories, photos, recipes, listings) remains yours — you grant Ami Goans a licence to display it on the platform. You're responsible for having the right to share anything you post.
  • We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these terms or our Community Guidelines.
  • Ami Goans is under active development. Features may change, and the service is provided without warranty while we build it out.

Community Guidelines

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech or abuse.
  • Only list real, Goan-owned or Goan-relevant businesses, events and jobs — no spam or fraudulent listings.
  • Share stories, photos and recipes that are genuinely yours or that you have permission to share.
  • Report anything that concerns you via Contribute or Contact Us.
