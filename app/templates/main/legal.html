Cookie Policy

Ami Goans uses only the cookies necessary to run the site:

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in between page visits. Deleted when you sign out or it expires.
  • CSRF token: a security measure to protect forms (like sign-up and profile updates) from being submitted by malicious third-party sites.

We do not currently use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies. (Our server does keep its own access logs, described in the Privacy Policy above — this is separate from cookies and isn't shared with any third party.)
