Goa Day — The UK Goan Festival

Goa Day, also known as the UK Goan Festival, is an annual celebration of Goan culture, food, music and faith, held at Cranford Community College in Hounslow, London, and organised by the Goan Association (UK). It's the largest yearly gathering of the UK Goan community, drawing thousands of visitors for a day of Mass, cultural performances, food stalls and live entertainment.

Exact dates vary year to year — check the Goan Association UK's Facebook page below for the current year's date, and see recordings on our Videos & Podcasts page.
